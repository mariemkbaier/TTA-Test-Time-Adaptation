\subsection{The learned representation contributes nothing to novelty detection}
\label{sec:os-depth}

The prototypes of Section~\ref{sec:os-maha} are built in the model's final $64$-dimensional
embedding, which is trained to separate three known activities and therefore to discard
whatever does not serve that. Whether novelty survives that compression is testable at no
cost, by building the same prototypes at three other depths, including none at all.

% source: src/exp27_proto_depth.py -> results/exp27_proto_depth.csv

The raw input features match the final embedding: $0.6392$ against $0.6377$ in one
direction and $0.5253$ against $0.5373$ in the other, differences well inside the seed
spread. The intermediate blocks are worse than both. Passing the window through a trained
network, in other words, neither adds novelty information nor destroys it; the distance
could as well be computed on the thirteen input features directly.

Two things follow. The first is practical: the rejection component of the deployable
configuration does not require the classifier at all, and could run beside a model of any
architecture, or none. The second is about why the detector is weak. If a representation
trained on the known classes carries no more novelty signal than the untrained input, then
the limit is not the compression but the features: thirteen layout-invariant window
statistics describe how much activity is occurring and when, and two activities can differ
in neither while being different activities. The withheld classes are not separable in
this description of the data, and no amount of depth recovers what the description never
encoded.
